# Snakes & Ladders mini-app (`games/snakes`)

- Free Play canvas engine (Aura) — classic 1–100 board, snakes/ladders, exact finish
- License/inspiration refs: [lemueldiergos/snake-and-ladder](https://github.com/lemueldiergos/snake-and-ladder) (MIT), [abp437/snake-and-ladders](https://github.com/abp437/snake-and-ladders) (MIT) — we ship a self-contained WebView build (no CDN assets)
- Pack: `node scripts/pack-snakes.mjs` → `mobile/src/runtime/snakesBundle.ts`
